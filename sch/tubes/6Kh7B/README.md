# 6Kh7B (6Х7Б, 6Х7Б-В, 6Х7Б-ВР): double diode

Reference data from the handbook pages the owner sent on 2026-10-29 (scans in `scan/`): the sheet of 6Х7Б / 6Х7Б-В / 6Х7Б-ВР (`6kh7b_sheet_params.png`, `6kh7b_sheet_limits.png`) and a smaller page with the drawing and the initial characteristic, Рис. 538–539 (`6kh7b_handbook_p1.png`). Model: `sch/libs/6Kh7B.sub` (one diode per subcircuit), generated by `tools/spice/fit_6kh7b.py`.

Use in the project:
- the asynchronous clear/preset: `sch/logic/DFFRN_J2N16X7`, `sch/logic/DFFSN_J2N16X7` (one tube per flip-flop);
- the `*_N16X7` diode cells of the liberty (not designed).

Purpose (handbook): double diode for detection and rectification. Oxide cathode, indirectly heated. Subminiature glass bulb, flexible leads. Mass 3.5 g (6Х7Б-ВР 4 g).

## Initial characteristic (Рис. 539)

The averaged initial anode characteristic with a 40 kΩ load, Ia against Ua from −0.6 to +0.4 V. A vacuum diode conducts at Ua = 0 and still at small negative Ua (contact potential, initial velocity of the electrons). The curve on the scan is too small to digitize; the model rests on the two numbers below.

## Model

`sch/libs/6Kh7B.sub`, pins `A K`:

    Ia = P · sp(Ua + VC)^1.5,   sp(x) = S · ln(1 + exp(x / S))

- Above about 0.5 V this is Child's law. Below it is the retarding-field region, Ia ∝ exp(Ua / VT), with VT = S / 1.5 = kT/q of an oxide cathode at about 1050 K (90.5 mV).
- **P** = 1.107 mA/V^1.5, from the emission bound (≥ 35 mA at 10 V; the model gives 39 mA). The real diode is at least this strong.
- **VC**, the contact potential, from the initial current. The file uses the bound: 20 µA at Ua = 0 into 40 kΩ (VC = 0.74 V, `--case leaky`). `--case nom` puts it at 5 µA (VC ≈ 0) for comparison.
- CAK = 5.8 pF (the maximum), 100 MΩ insulation. The heater is not modelled.

This is a model from bounds, not a fit to a curve: good enough to size reverse bias and currents, not for detector work. Real tubes should be measured (Ia at Ua = −1 … +1 V).

## Result: the reset diode on a latch feedback node

`DFF_J2N16` reset idea (owner, 2026-10-29): a diode from the reset line (anode) to FQN, the grid of the cross-coupled triode on plate Q of each RS latch (`sch/logic/DFF_J2N16/README.md`). DC operating points, ngspice, one diode, node fed from QN through 470k / 200k to −50 V, triode with Ra 10k:

| Model | QN plate | Line | FQN | Line current | Plate Q |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| leaky | 55 V ("0", triode only) | −20 V | −18.65 V (+51 mV) | 0.05 µA | 150 V |
| leaky | 36 V ("0", both on) | −20 V | **−19.27 V** (was −24.36) | 36 µA | 150 V |
| any | 55 / 36 V | −50 V | −18.70 / −24.36 V | 0.3 µA (insulation) | 150 V |
| leaky / nom | 150 V ("1") | −20 or −50 V | +0.42 V | 0.2–0.5 µA | 55 V |
| leaky / nom | 55 V | **0 V (reset)** | +0.43 / −0.22 V | 0.21 / 0.13 mA | **55 / 67 V** |
| leaky / nom | 36 V | 0 V (reset) | +0.40 / −0.27 V | 0.23 / 0.17 mA | 56 / 68 V |
| leaky | 55 V | +10 V (reset) | +8.3 V | — | 7 V: the triode grid draws mA, too hard |

- **Idle at −20 V works.** With QN at 55 V the node moves 51 mV. With QN at 36 V the node sits below the line, so the diode clamps it at −19.3 V and takes 36 µA. The node is still 17 V under the triode's switching point (−2.2 V), so the latch is not disturbed. Idle at −50 V leaves every node untouched and is an existing rail. −20 V is not, and the rail would have to sink up to ~36 µA per diode.
- **Reset level 0 V (GND), not +10 V.** At 0 V the node goes to −0.3 … +0.4 V and plate Q to 55–68 V, a valid "0" (next stage threshold 92 V). +10 V forces the grid into heavy grid current.
- **Line current while reset is held**: 0.13–0.23 mA per diode, two diodes per flop: 10–18 mA for 38 flops.
- Not checked yet: the transient effect of the 5.8 pF A–K capacitance on the feedback node (it under-compensates the 3.9 pF divider), and the release against C. This belongs in the DFFR cell's sweep.
