The machine is the golden model dpcrun ported to JavaScript, instruction for instruction. It counts retired instructions (IRET), not clock cycles. Programs load through the bootloader ROM over SOT/EOT; data memory is cleared on power-on, on a new program load and with the MEM CLEAN key. AP wraps at 99999, as in the current RTL (OPEN-001).

    
STEP pauses the simulator clock after one instruction. HALT goes through the machine's halt path, like the RTL key: right after a taken bracket it skips the loop scan. Brainfuck-100 programs are from bfutils/programs/bf100, each under its own license. Source: radiolok/dekatronpc.
